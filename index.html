MacTrace

Free, keyboard-driven formula tracing for Excel on Mac.

Ever inherited a spreadsheet and wondered where does this number come from? MacTrace breaks any formula into a readable tree, shows the live value and location of every cell it references, and lets you jump straight to any of them — even on another sheet. It also shows every cell that depends on your selection, so you can change things without breaking the workbook. All from the keyboard.

Download for Mac

Free & open source · Excel for Mac 16.55 (late 2021) or newer · No Microsoft 365 subscription needed

How to install

  1. Download the installer with the button above, then open it from your Downloads folder.
  2. If your Mac says it can't verify the developer (that's normal — MacTrace is a free community project, not registered with Apple): open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click “Open Anyway” next to the MacTrace message, then confirm.
    On older Macs (before macOS Sequoia): right-click the downloaded file, choose Open, then Open again.
  3. Follow the installer — it takes a few seconds, needs no password, and will close and reopen Excel for you (save your work first). Then look on Excel's Formulas tab for the Trace and Dependents buttons, or just press Cmd+Option+A. Done!
    MacTrace not showing, or Cmd+Option+A does nothing? In Excel, choose Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins…. That loads MacTrace.

Good to know

Private by design. MacTrace runs inside Excel on your Mac and reads your formulas locally. Your spreadsheet data never leaves your computer — the internet connection is only used to load the add-in itself.

Always up to date. Improvements arrive automatically; there is nothing to update or maintain.

Easy to remove. Delete one file and restart Excel: in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G, paste ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Excel/Data/Documents/wef and delete MacTrace.hosted.manifest.xml.

MacTrace is free and open source — view the code on GitHub.
